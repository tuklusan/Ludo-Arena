Ludo AI Arena

Watch four computer players - HAL 9000, Marvin, Mal and Deckard - play a complete game of Ludo in your browser: animated dice, captures, blockades and bonus rolls. Press the screen to start; no installation and no sign-up.

This browser version uses built-in strategy bots and makes no AI-model or network calls. The desktop app for Linux, Windows and macOS adds language-model players through NVIDIA NIM.
