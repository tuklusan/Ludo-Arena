=========================================================
Each image is the FINAL board frame of one complete game (first roll to declared
winner), rendered by the application itself via Avalonia RenderTargetBitmap.
The winning player's card reads Done:4 and is highlighted.

Common to every image:

  Identical source, no platform-specific code.
